# AI Coding Rules (Andrej Karpathy Style)

1. **Think before you code:** Do not make blind assumptions. If the context or instruction is unclear, ask for clarification before writing any code.
2. **Keep it simple:** Implement only what is strictly requested. If 100 lines are enough, do not write 1000 lines. Avoid overcomplicating code, adding bloated abstractions, or over-engineering.
3. **Surgical changes:** Do not touch or refactor unrelated code. Only modify what is absolutely necessary to accomplish the current task.
4. **Goal-driven & Test-first:** When given a specific success criteria or test, loop and iterate autonomously until the goal is met or the tests pass. Do not just explain what you are going to do; execute it and verify.-
